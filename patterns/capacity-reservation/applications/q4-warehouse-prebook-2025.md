# Q4 warehouse pre-book, 2026

A regional Q4 push had historically paid a forty-percent
contention premium for outbound warehouse slots in November
and December. The pattern was applied to pre-book sixty
percent of expected slot need in July at the off-window rate.

The reservation captured the headline saving on the share that
was used. The friction came from the sizing: actual demand
came in below forecast, leaving fifteen percent of the
reservation unused and non-refundable. The net effect was
still positive against the spot baseline, but the unused share
ate into the saving more than the canonical analysis
predicted. The lesson logged back in the pattern body is that
"sized close to actual need" is the load-bearing phrase, and a
sixty-percent reservation off a soft forecast is closer to the
upper bound than the canonical statement implies.
