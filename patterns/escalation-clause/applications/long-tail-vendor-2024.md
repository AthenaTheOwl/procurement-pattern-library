# Long-tail vendor escalation, 2026

A long-tail vendor contract was rewritten to include a price-
escalation clause keyed to a published commodity index. The
clause named the buyer-side commercial lead by role, capped
the upward adjustment per quarter, and set a fifteen-business-
day window for the review.

The clause fired twice in the following twelve months. Both
times the named owner closed the review inside the fifteen-day
window, and the capped adjustment was applied without
contention. The pattern transferred cleanly: the pre-
negotiated path absorbed the exception without producing a
re-negotiation, exactly as the canonical statement predicts.
