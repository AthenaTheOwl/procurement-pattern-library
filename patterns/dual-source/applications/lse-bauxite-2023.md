# LSE bauxite dual-source, 2026 carry-over

The procurement-side instance the pattern is named after. A
single bauxite supplier had been the sole feed into the LSE
contract for three years; a routine audit surfaced that the
supplier was itself single-sourced from one mine.

The decision opened a second contract with a smaller supplier
at a fifteen-percent volume share. The redundancy cost was
quantified at the smaller supplier's price premium. Within
twelve months the primary suspended deliveries during a
permitting dispute; the secondary absorbed full volume for six
weeks at the contracted price ceiling. The pattern transferred
cleanly: the predicted failure mode was the realized failure
mode, and the secondary worked on first use because the
fifteen-percent share kept it warm.
